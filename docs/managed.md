# Self-hosted or managed

codx-junior can be used in two ways.

## Self-host the engine for free

The whole platform is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. Run it on your own infrastructure with Docker Compose, connect the AI providers you trust, and keep your code where it already is. Follow [Getting started](/getting-started).

## Hire our managed team

Prefer to focus on the product? Our team of veteran developers works inside codx-junior to execute your roadmap:

* You keep full visibility on the Kanban board, the chats and every change.
* AI agents do the volume; our developers review, steer and ship.
* Your budget is transparent: **human hours** on one side and **AI tokens** on the other, both visible in the analytics dashboard.
