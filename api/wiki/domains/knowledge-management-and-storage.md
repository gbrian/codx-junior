# Knowledge Management and Storage

## Overview
The **Knowledge Management and Storage** domain serves as the foundational architectural layer for data persistence and cognitive intelligence within the Codx-Junior system. It is responsible for bridging the gap between raw structured data and actionable AI-driven insights. 

By integrating robust database management practices with advanced vector-based embedding models, this domain enables the system to store, categorize, and retrieve information with high semantic relevance. It powers the retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) capabilities and ensures that the knowledge base remains consistent, scalable, and readily accessible for AI inference.
