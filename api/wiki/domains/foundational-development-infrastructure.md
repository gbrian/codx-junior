# Foundational Development Infrastructure

## Overview
This foundational infrastructure domain manages the critical meta-data, operational licenses, and structural guidelines necessary for developing complex software applications. It functions as the bedrock layer that establishes the legal and architectural boundaries of a project. This domain ensures mandatory compliance with intellectual property laws from the outset, guaranteeing clarity regarding usage rights (e.g., open source vs. proprietary) before coding commences.

The components within this domain cover:
*   **Licensing Documentation:** Providing legally binding terms (represented by `LICENSE.md`).
*   **Project Dependencies Management:** Establishing formal rules for how modules interact and rely on each other, ensuring long-term maintainability and legal compatibility.
*   **Operational Meta-data:** Containing information on ownership, version control mandates, and contributor obligations.

Effective management of this domain is non-negotiable, as it governs the scope and viability of all subsequent module development efforts within the larger codex-junior ecosystem. This layer minimizes future legal risk and standardizes dependency usage across various internal projects.
